Uses of the Infinitive

1.  DYNAMIC \[CGCG 51.8-18\]

    a.  Complementary

        i.  Necessary to complete the meaning of the verb \[OBJ\]

        ii. It does not usually have its own subject

        iii. Negated by μή

        iv. With modals, verbs of knowing, intending, wishing, ordering,
            being able, forcing, learning, etc. \[κελεύω is very
            common\]

    b.  Subject of Impersonal constructions

    c.  Epexegetical Infinitive \[explanatory\]

        i.  May modify verbs to express purpose \[ADV\] \[51.16\]

        ii. May modify adjectives or nouns, like the accusative of
            respect \[51.9\]\[ADV/ATR\]

2.  DECLARATIVE \[CGCG 51.19-27\]

    a.  Indirect Statements

    b.  Infinitive acts as OBJ of the verb of saying, thinking,
        believing, etc.

    c.  Has its own subject in accusative

3.  ARTICULAR/Substantive \[CGCG 51.38-46\]

    a.  Use neuter singular article: τό, τοῦ, τῷ, τό \[sometimes
        omitted\]

    b.  Treat the entire infinitive clause as a noun/substantive

4.  Result Clauses after ὥστε = potential result = ADV \[ch. 46\]
