**[Common Coordinating Conjunctions]{.ul}**

-   καί or τε \[enclitic\] or τε καί, etc. ("and")

-   τε \... καί or τε ... τε \... τε, or καί ... καί, or virtually any
    combination

-   οὐδέ ("and not" or "not even"); οὐδέ ... οὐδέ... ("neither ...
    nor...")

    -   οὔτε \... οὔτε \... ("neither ... nor...")

-   δέ ("and" usually used to join parallel clauses or sentences)

-   ἀλλά ("and" usually used to join parallel clauses or sentences)

-   μέν \... δέ (implies a contrast: "on the one hand...on the other..."

    -   οἱ μὲν ..., οἱ δέ... ("Some people.... other people...")

-   ἤ \... ἤ ("either ... or...)
