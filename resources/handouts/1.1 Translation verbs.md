2.1.3.3 Translation: Basic Verbs

1.  Thrasyllos sailed.

> NOTE: "Thrasyllos" is the subject in the nominative singular
> masculine.

2.  Kallixenos was sending.

3.  Thymochares was doing.

4.  Thymochares took.

5.  Kallixenos was sailing.

6.  Thrasyllos acted unjustly.

7.  The Peloponnesians were making.

> NOTE: "Peloponnesians" is the nominative plural masculine SUBJ. Ethnic
> words like this can be used as nouns or as adjectives.

8.  Pharnabazos was fighting.

9.  The Peloponnesians were fighting.

10. Theramenes and Kritias fought.

> NOTE: καί = "and."

11. Hippokrates died.

12. Theramenes was dying.

13. They spoke.

> NOTE: since no subject is expressed, derive the subject pronouns from
> the person and number of the verb. Here, 3rd PL = "they."

14. Thymochares came.

15. They did wrong.

16. Tissaphernes was ruling.

17. The Lakedaimonians were sending.

18. Thymochares was waging war.

19. Kritias acted.

20. They killed \[him\].

NOTE: Sometimes one must not only derive the subject from the verb, but
also assume an object from the context in the sentence. If an OBJ is
required but not stated, one must identify it from the surrounding
sentences.

> NOTE: Because the verb is copular and only one nominative (the PNOM)
