Possessives adjectives

-   

> ἐμός, ἐμή, ἐμόν = *my*
>
> σός, σή, σόν = *your, yours* (singular)
>
> ἡμέτερος, -τέρα, τερον = *our, ours*
>
> ὑμέτερος, -τέρα, τερον = *your, yours* (plural)
>
> σφέτερος, -τέρα, τερον = *their*

Summary of Verbs Moods in Independent Clauses

-   Imperative Mood. "Go to war!" in the 2^nd^ and 3^rd^ person

    Independent Optative \[note: to *opt for* something is to *wish for*
    it\]

    -   Potential (with ἄν). "We might go to war."

        Wish (without ἄν). "May we go to war!"

    Independent Subjunctive \[unreal\]

    -   Hortatory in 1^st^ person \[= commands in 1^st^ person\] "Let us
        go to war."

        Deliberative in 1^st^ person [questions]{.ul}. "Should we go to
        war?"

        Prohibitive in 2^nd^ person (with μή). "Do not go to war!"
