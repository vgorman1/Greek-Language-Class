Greek Particles translated with gestures

μέν - lean vaguely to one side

δέ - lean more vigorously to the other side

γε - hit the table lightly with your fist

ἄρα - gesture vaguely with one hand

δή - nod once, firmly

μήν - hold up both hands

ἦ - "Hey!" Hold up both hands

οὖν - gesture vaguely with one hand

γάρ - "'cuz"

τοί - "y'know"

μέντοι - raise one finger (any finger)

καίτοι - waggle a finger

τοίνυν - slap back of hand into other hand

δήπου - add a Canadian-style "...eh?" to the end of the sentence
