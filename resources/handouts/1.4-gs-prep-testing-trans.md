Gold Standard Sentences

GS (testing): 80, 81, 90, 91, 92, 122, 131, 137

1.  They made oaths, contrary to the law.

2.  The polemarch paid the peltasts.

NOTE: Literally, "gave pay to."

3.  Pasippidas the Lakonian was fleeing from Sparta.

4.  Eutyptolemos spoke concerning the generals.

5.  Hieramenes and his brother were speaking to Darius.

6.  They were besieging \[him\] by land and by sea.

7.  Tissaphernes and Derkylidas and the barbarians were living in peace.

NOTE: βάρβαρος has no connotation of "uncivilized" or "culturally
backwards." It was a catch-all term meaning anyone who did not speak
Greek. In particular, it was applied to the Persians.

8.  The ones at home stopped \[deposed\] the generals except Konon.

NOTE: The preposition here limits the scope of "generals" and so acts as
an ATR. --The verb παύω is cognate with English pause.
